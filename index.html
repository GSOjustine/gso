Explore Features

Discover what the GSO Custodian Inventory System can do for your office.

  • Dashboard Overview

    Real-time snapshot of total items, ICS records, PAR records, and total asset value.

  • Inventory Custodian Slip

    Create, edit, and search ICS records with quantity, unit, description, item number, useful life, and price.

  • Property Acknowledgment Receipt

    Manage PAR records with property number, date acquired, and amount — fully searchable and editable.

  • One-Click Document Export

    Download individual or batch ICS and PAR records as formatted Word documents with ready signature blocks.

  • Instant Search & Filter

    Quickly find any record by typing keywords — filters apply live as you type across all fields.

  • Automatic Totals

    Total items, record counts, and total monetary value update automatically on every change.

  • Secure Custodian Access

    Login-protected dashboard ensures only authorized custodians can view and manage inventory data.

  • Local Data Persistence

    All records are saved automatically in your browser — data stays available even after closing the tab.

  • Official Document Layouts

    Exports follow the official LGU format with header, entity details, and leveled signature sections.

  • Responsive Design

    Works smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile — the dashboard, tables, and modals adapt to any screen size.

Vision

A progressive, efficient, and well-organized General Services Office committed to providing quality support services through effective management of government properties, supplies, equipment, facilities, and resources, contributing to a responsive, transparent, and service-oriented local government.

Mission

To ensure the efficient, transparent, and accountable management, procurement, safekeeping, maintenance, utilization, and disposal of government properties, supplies, equipment, and facilities; to provide timely and reliable logistical support to all municipal offices; and to promote the proper stewardship of public resources for effective local governance and quality public service delivery.
